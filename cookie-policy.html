Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and provide a better browsing experience. Cookies cannot run programs or deliver viruses to your computer — they are simply data files.

2. How FreebieCalculator.com Uses Cookies

FreebieCalculator.com is a static, client-side website. We use a minimal number of cookies and browser storage technologies, described below.

2.1 Essential / Functional Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are set in response to actions you take, such as setting your dark/light mode preference.

NamePurposeDuration
themeStores your dark/light mode preferencePersistent (localStorage)
recentlyViewedRemembers recently used calculators for quick accessPersistent (localStorage)

Note: The above use localStorage, not actual HTTP cookies. They are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Advertising Cookies (Third-Party)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may place cookies on your device to serve personalized ads based on your interests and browsing history across websites. These cookies are set by Google's domains and are subject to Google's own cookie and privacy policies.

  • Provider: Google LLC
  • Purpose: Ad personalization and frequency capping
  • Duration: Up to 13 months (varies by cookie)
  • Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Opt-Out: adssettings.google.com

2.3 Analytics Cookies (Future)

We may add Google Analytics in the future to understand how visitors use the site. If and when analytics is enabled, this policy will be updated accordingly. No analytics cookies are currently active.

3. Third-Party Cookies

When you visit FreebieCalculator.com, third-party services (such as Google AdSense) may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of these third parties for more information.

4. Your Cookie Choices

Browser Settings

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. Most web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through the browser's settings. Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: Blocking all cookies may impact the functionality of FreebieCalculator.com (e.g., your theme preference may not be saved between visits).

Opting Out of Personalized Ads

To opt out of interest-based advertising by Google, visit Google Ad Settings. You can also opt out via the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org.

5. localStorage and sessionStorage

In addition to cookies, FreebieCalculator.com uses the browser's localStorage to save your preferences (such as theme selection and recently viewed calculators). This data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear localStorage via your browser's developer tools or by clearing browsing data.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. FreebieCalculator.com does not currently respond to DNT signals. However, we collect very little data by design and do not sell personal data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us via our Contact page.
