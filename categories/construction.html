How to Use These Calculators

Enter your room or area dimensions and the calculators automatically add a standard waste factor (typically 10–15%) so you order enough material without significant overstock. All formulas follow industry-standard guidelines used by professional contractors.

Results are estimates — always verify with your supplier and account for site-specific factors before purchasing materials.
