About Our Health Calculators

Our health calculators use clinically validated formulas such as the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for BMR, the Deurenberg formula for body fat, and WHO guidelines for BMI classification. Results are educational estimates — individual variation exists, and these tools do not replace professional medical evaluation.
