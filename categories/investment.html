How Investment Projections Work

Our investment calculators use standard financial formulas with fixed rate assumptions. Real investment returns vary due to market fluctuations, fees, taxes, and timing. These tools are best used for scenario planning and education — not as guaranteed forecasts. Always diversify and consider consulting a registered financial advisor for personalized guidance.
