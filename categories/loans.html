How Loan Calculations Work

Our loan calculators use the standard amortization formula: P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1] where P is the principal, r is the monthly rate, and n is the number of payments. This matches the calculations used by banks and lenders, so you can reliably compare offers before applying.
