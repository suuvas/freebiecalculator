Overtime Pay Calculator

Calculate your total weekly pay including regular and overtime hours. Supports time-and-a-half, double time, and custom multipliers.

💰 Financial Disclaimer: This calculator is for educational purposes only. Consult a qualified financial professional.

How It Works

Multiplies regular hours by rate, then overtime hours by rate times multiplier, and sums both amounts.

Formula

Total = (Regular Hours × Rate) + (OT Hours × Rate × Multiplier)

Example

$20/hr, 40 reg + 5 OT hours, 1.5×:
Regular $800 + OT $150 = $950 total

When to Use This Calculator

Use after each pay period to verify your paycheck, or when negotiating overtime terms with your employer.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Assuming all OT is 1.5× — check your employment contract.
  • Not tracking hours — keep a time log for documentation.
  • Forgetting bonuses are usually excluded from the OT base rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who qualifies for overtime in the US?
Non-exempt employees working over 40 hours/week must receive at least 1.5× their regular rate under FLSA.
Is overtime taxed differently?
Overtime is taxed at the same income rate. It may push you into a higher bracket for that period.
What is double time?
2× your regular rate. California requires double time for hours over 12 in a single day.
Does PTO count toward 40 hours?
No — only actual hours worked count toward the overtime threshold.
