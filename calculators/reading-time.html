Reading Time Calculator

Find out exactly how long any content will take to read. Paste text or enter word count and select your reading speed.

How It Works

Divides word count by reading speed (words per minute). Paste text to auto-count words.

Formula

Reading Time = Word Count ÷ Words Per Minute
Average: 200–300 wpm | Technical: 100–150 wpm | Fiction: 300+ wpm

Example

1,500-word article at 250 wpm:
1,500 ÷ 250 = 6 minutes

When to Use This Calculator

Use to estimate reading assignment time, plan study sessions, or estimate audio recording lengths.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using average speed for technical content — dense academic text is often 100–150 wpm.
  • Not accounting for comprehension time — reading speed excludes pausing to process complex concepts.
  • Applying wpm to audio — audiobooks average 150–200 wpm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Average adult reading speed?
200–300 wpm for general non-fiction. Skilled readers: 400–600 wpm.
Words per book page?
Average page: 250–300 words. 300-page book ≈ 75,000–90,000 words.
Time for a research paper?
3,000–5,000 words: 15–30 minutes to read; 1–2 hours to study carefully.
Does audiobook listening count as reading?
For comprehension yes. Most people comprehend well at 1.5–2× speed.
