Understanding Amortization

📈 Payment Breakdown

Each payment contains both principal and interest. Early payments are mostly interest, while later payments are mostly principal.

📊 Schedule Benefits

See exactly when you'll pay off your loan, how much interest you'll pay each year, and how extra payments can save money.

💰 Interest Savings

Use the schedule to identify the best times to make extra principal payments for maximum interest savings.

When to Use This Calculator

Use this when you have an existing loan and want to see exactly how much of each payment goes to principal vs interest. It helps you understand the total interest cost over the loan life and evaluate the impact of making extra payments.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring extra payment impact — even $50/month extra can save thousands in interest and years off the loan.
  • Confusing APR with interest rate — use the stated interest rate (not APR) in the amortization formula.
  • Not recalculating after refinancing — your old amortization schedule becomes invalid after a rate change.
