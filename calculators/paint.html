Paint Calculator

Calculate exactly how much paint to buy — walls, ceiling, doors, and windows accounted for. Includes 10% waste.

How It Works

Computes wall area (perimeter × height), subtracts openings, divides by coverage (350 sq ft/gallon), multiplies by coats, adds 10% waste.

Formula

Wall Area = 2(L+W) × H − Openings
Gallons = Wall Area × Coats ÷ 350 × 1.10

Example

12×14 ft room, 8 ft ceilings, 2 windows, 1 door, 2 coats:
Wall area = 366 sq ft. Gallons = 2.3 (incl. waste)

When to Use This Calculator

Use before any painting project to buy the right amount and avoid costly return trips for color-matched paint.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • No waste factor — buy 10% extra minimum.
  • One coat over dark colors — dark-to-light needs primer + 2 finish coats.
  • Forgetting ceiling — calculate separately if different color.

Frequently Asked Questions

Coverage per gallon?
350–400 sq ft on previously painted surfaces. Rough/unpainted walls: 200–300 sq ft.
Do I need primer?
Use over bare drywall, going dark to light, or covering stains.
How long between coats?
Latex paint: 2–4 hours. Wait overnight for best results.
Interior vs exterior paint?
Never interchange — each has specialized additives for its environment.
