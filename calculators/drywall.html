Drywall Calculator

Calculate the exact number of 4×8 ft drywall sheets for your project — walls and ceiling with all openings deducted.

How It Works

Calculates wall area minus openings, adds ceiling if selected, applies 10% waste, and divides by 32 sq ft per sheet.

Formula

Wall Area = 2(L+W) × H − Openings
Sheets = (Walls + Ceiling) × 1.10 ÷ 32

Example

12×14 ft room, 8 ft ceiling, 2 windows, 1 door, with ceiling:
~534 sq ft × 1.10 / 32 = 19 sheets

When to Use This Calculator

Use before any drywall renovation or new construction to order the correct quantity.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • No corner waste budget — add 10% minimum.
  • Wrong thickness — 1/2" standard interior; 5/8" ceilings and fire-rated.
  • Ordering exact count — always round up to full sheets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Drywall thickness?
1/2" standard walls; 5/8" ceilings and fire-rated; 3/8" curved surfaces.
Screws per sheet?
~32 screws per 4×8 sheet (every 12" into studs at 16" OC).
DIY drywall?
Walls: manageable solo. Ceilings: much easier with 2 people or a drywall lift.
Cost?
$12–$20 per 1/2" 4×8 sheet. Specialty types cost more.
