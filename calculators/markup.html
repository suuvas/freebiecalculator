Markup Calculator

Set prices confidently. Enter cost and markup % to find selling price, or enter cost and price to find markup %.

How It Works

Markup is % of cost added to find selling price. Calculates in both directions: price from markup, or markup from price.

Formula

Selling Price = Cost × (1 + Markup/100)
Markup % = (Price − Cost) / Cost × 100

Example

Cost $25, markup 80%:
Price = $25 × 1.80 = $45. Gross margin = 44.4%.

When to Use This Calculator

Use when pricing wholesale-to-retail products, creating a standard pricing policy, or verifying prices against cost targets.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing markup with margin — 50% markup ≠ 50% margin.
  • Not covering all costs — include indirect and overhead.
  • Same markup for all products — commodity items need lower markup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Standard retail markup?
Keystone = 100% markup (50% margin). Modern retail: 50–80%.
Markup vs margin relationship?
100% markup = 50% margin. 50% markup = 33% margin. 25% markup = 20% margin.
Restaurant markup?
Typically 300%+ on food cost (food cost = 25–33% of menu price).
Can markup be negative?
Yes — selling below cost is negative markup (loss leader strategy).
