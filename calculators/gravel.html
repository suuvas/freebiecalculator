Gravel Calculator

Calculate cubic yards and tons of gravel needed for driveways, garden paths, French drains, and landscaping beds.

How It Works

Computes volume in cubic feet, converts to cubic yards, and estimates weight in tons based on material density.

Formula

Volume (cu ft) = L × W × Depth
Cu Yards = Volume ÷ 27
Tons = Cu Yds × Density

Example

Driveway 40×12 ft, 3 in deep:
120 cu ft = 4.44 cu yds. Tons: 4.44 × 1.4 = 6.2 tons

When to Use This Calculator

Use before ordering bulk gravel delivery. Gravel is sold by the cubic yard or ton; suppliers need both.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Depth in feet not inches — calculator expects inches; 3 in = 0.25 ft.
  • Not adding 10% buffer — settling and spreading losses are common.
  • Confusing cu yds with cu ft — 1 cu yd = 27 cu ft.

Frequently Asked Questions

Driveway depth?
4–6 inches minimum. 3-layer system (6" base + 4" mid + 2" surface) is ideal.
Tons per cubic yard?
Standard gravel: 1.35–1.5 t/cu yd. Use 1.4 for estimates.
Haul myself?
Pickup truck safely carries ~1 ton (0.7 cu yd). Large projects: bulk delivery.
Best gravel for drainage?
Pea gravel (3/8") for surface; clean 3/4" crushed stone for French drains.
