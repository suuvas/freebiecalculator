Flooring Calculator

Calculate exactly how much flooring to order for any room, including waste for cuts and difficult installation areas.

How It Works

Calculates room area (length × width), applies waste factor, and shows results in sq ft, sq yds, and sq meters.

Formula

Area = L × W
Order Qty = Area × (1 + Waste%/100)

Example

15×12 ft room = 180 sq ft. 10% waste: 198 sq ft (22 sq yds)

When to Use This Calculator

Use before purchasing any flooring material to ensure you buy enough without major overages.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying the exact calculated amount — you will likely run short.
  • Confusing sq yards with sq feet — verify pricing units.
  • Forgetting closets — measure all spaces and add together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Extra for diagonal installation?
15% for 45° diagonal vs 10% for straight installation.
L-shaped room?
Divide into rectangles, calculate each, add together.
Buy extra for repairs?
Yes — buy 5–10% extra; future batches may not match color exactly.
Easiest DIY flooring?
Click-lock luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and laminate are most beginner-friendly.
