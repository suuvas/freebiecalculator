Tile Calculator

Calculate exactly how many tiles to buy for any tiling project, including a waste factor for cuts and breakage.

How It Works

Divides total area by each tile's face area and adds the chosen waste factor for cuts at edges and breakage.

Formula

Area = L × W
Tiles = Area ÷ (Tile L × Tile W) × (1 + Waste%)

Example

12×12 ft floor, 12×12 in tiles, 10% waste:
144 sq ft ÷ 1 sq ft × 1.10 = 159 tiles

When to Use This Calculator

Use before purchasing tiles for floors, walls, or shower surrounds to ensure you buy enough.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping waste factor — add 10% minimum; 15–20% for diagonal patterns.
  • Forgetting grout lines — slightly underestimates tile count.
  • Buying from different batches later — production batches vary in shade.

Frequently Asked Questions

Extra tile to buy?
10% straight, 15% diagonal, 20% herringbone or complex patterns.
Grout joint size?
1/16" rectified tiles, 3/16" standard floor tiles, 3/8"+ natural stone.
Calculate in sq meters?
Enter dimensions in cm, keep all units consistent.
DIY installation time?
Bathroom: 1–2 days. Full kitchen floor: 3–5 days plus drying time.
