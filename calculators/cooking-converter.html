Cooking Converter

Convert any cooking measurement instantly — cups to milliliters, tablespoons to teaspoons, ounces to grams, and more.

How It Works

Uses standard conversion ratios between all common cooking volume and weight units.

Formula

1 cup = 16 tbsp = 48 tsp = 237 ml = 8 fl oz
1 oz = 28.35g | 1 lb = 453.6g

Example

2 cups = 2 × 237 ml = 474 ml

When to Use This Calculator

Use when following international recipes, scaling recipes up or down, or converting between US and metric measurements.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Assuming all ingredients weigh the same per cup — 1 cup flour ≠ 1 cup sugar by weight.
  • Confusing fluid ounces with weight ounces — fl oz is volume; oz is weight.
  • UK pint (568 ml) vs US pint (473 ml) — significant in baking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tablespoons in a cup?
1 cup = 16 tablespoons = 48 teaspoons.
ml per teaspoon?
1 US tsp = 4.93 ml, typically rounded to 5 ml.
Cups to grams?
Depends on ingredient: water 237g/cup; flour ~125g/cup; sugar ~200g/cup.
US cup vs metric cup?
US = 236.6 ml. Metric (AU/CA) = 250 ml. Matters in precision baking.
