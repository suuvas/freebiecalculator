Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

Our Commitment

FreebieCalculator.com is committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards as outlined by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

We believe that everyone deserves access to free, accurate calculation tools — regardless of ability or the assistive technology they use.

Conformance Status

FreebieCalculator.com is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard. We are actively working to improve conformance across all pages and calculators.

Accessibility Features

We have implemented the following features to improve accessibility:

  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive elements — forms, buttons, links, and menus — are accessible via keyboard alone (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space).
  • Screen Reader Support: Pages use semantic HTML5 elements with proper heading hierarchy (H1–H4), ARIA labels, and ARIA landmark roles to support screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver.
  • Color Contrast: Text and interactive elements are designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios (at least 4.5:1 for normal text).
  • Dark Mode: A built-in dark mode reduces visual strain for users with light sensitivity or who prefer low-contrast environments.
  • Resizable Text: The site uses relative font units (rem/em) so text can be resized up to 200% in browser settings without loss of content or functionality.
  • Form Labels: All form inputs have associated visible labels and are programmatically associated using for/id attributes.
  • Error Identification: Form validation errors are communicated clearly in text, not color alone.
  • Skip Navigation: A skip-to-content link is available at the top of each page for keyboard users.
  • ARIA Live Regions: Calculator results are announced to screen readers via ARIA live regions when they are updated.
  • No Flashing Content: The site contains no content that flashes more than three times per second.
  • Mobile Accessibility: Touch targets meet minimum size requirements, and the site is fully usable with mobile accessibility features such as iOS VoiceOver and Android TalkBack.

Known Limitations

While we work toward full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, some known limitations currently exist:

  • Some older calculator pages may have form fields without explicit visible error messages — we are updating these progressively.
  • Third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense may not fully meet our accessibility standards. We cannot control the content or accessibility of third-party ad content.
  • Complex chart or visualization outputs (where present) may not have complete text alternatives — we are working to add these.

Technical Specifications

FreebieCalculator.com relies on the following technologies for conformance:

  • HTML5
  • CSS3 (including CSS Custom Properties for theming)
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6 modules)
  • WAI-ARIA 1.1

These technologies are relied upon for conformance with WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria.

Browser and Assistive Technology Compatibility

We aim for compatibility with the following combinations:

  • Chrome + NVDA (Windows)
  • Firefox + NVDA (Windows)
  • Safari + VoiceOver (macOS and iOS)
  • Chrome + TalkBack (Android)
  • Edge + JAWS (Windows)

Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you experience any barriers or difficulties accessing content on FreebieCalculator.com, we want to hear from you. Please contact us by:

  • Using our Contact page

When reporting an issue, it helps to include:

  • The URL of the page where you encountered the problem
  • A description of the barrier you experienced
  • The browser and assistive technology you are using

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days and to resolve issues within 30 days wherever feasible.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact your national accessibility authority. In the United States, accessibility complaints related to websites can be submitted to the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division.

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review our site regularly using both automated tools (such as axe and Lighthouse) and manual testing with assistive technologies. We update our practices as WCAG guidelines evolve and as we receive feedback from our users.
