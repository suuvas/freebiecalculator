How We Use Sources

Every formula on FreebieCalculator.com is traced back to a primary authoritative source. We prioritize government agencies, peer-reviewed research, and recognized professional standards. Where a formula has multiple accepted versions (such as BMR equations), we document which version we use and why.

If you notice a discrepancy between our calculation and a source you believe is authoritative, please contact us at bugs@freebiecalculator.com with the reference link. We review all reports carefully.

Reporting a Source Error

If you believe we have misapplied a formula or cited an incorrect source, please email bugs@freebiecalculator.com with:

  • The calculator name and URL
  • The specific formula or value you believe is incorrect
  • A link to the authoritative source that supports your correction

We take source accuracy seriously and will respond within 5 business days.
